Privacy policy

e-GP Browser (Google Play edition) · Publisher: {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} · Effective: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

e-GP Browser is an independent browser for the public e-GP portal, www.eprocure.gov.bd. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Bangladesh, BPPA, CPTU or the e-GP portal.

What we collect

Nothing. This edition has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. The publisher receives no personal data from the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Cookies, cache, history and site storage of the e-GP portal, so that you stay signed in to the portal. You can clear them at any time (menu → Clear browsing data).
  • Files you download, saved in Downloads/e-GP.
  • Your app settings (for example desktop mode and text size).

The app never stores your e-GP password. Sign-in happens on the official portal page. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Data you send to the e-GP portal

Everything you type or upload in the browser is sent directly to www.eprocure.gov.bd over an encrypted connection and is governed by the portal's own terms and privacy policy. The app does not read, copy or send it anywhere else.

Services provided by Android

  • Google Safe Browsing (part of Android System WebView) checks visited addresses against lists of dangerous sites to protect you.
  • File picker and Downloads are system components; the app only receives the files you choose.

Permissions

Internet and network state (to load the portal and show offline status). On Android 8–9 only, storage permission to save downloads. No camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Children

The app is intended for professional procurement use and is not directed at children.
