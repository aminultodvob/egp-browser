e-GP Browser

Privacy policy

e-GP Browser · Publisher: egp-browser · Effective: 2026-10-29

e-GP Browser is an independent browser for the public e-GP portal, www.eprocure.gov.bd. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Bangladesh, BPPA, CPTU or the e-GP portal.

1. Data we collect and why

  • Account (when you sign in with Google): your Google account identifier, email address and name. Purpose: to keep your free trial and lifetime access with you across reinstalls and phones.
  • Trial records: when your 7-day trial started and ends. To stop the trial being restarted, we also keep a keyed one-way hash of your Google identifier and of your device's app-specific Android ID. These hashes cannot be turned back into the original values.
  • Payments: invoice number, amount (৳250), status, time, the payment method name and PayStation's transaction ID. Your name, mobile number and email are passed to PayStation to create the checkout; we do not store your mobile number. We never receive your PIN, OTP or card details.
  • Session tokens: encrypted sign-in tokens on your phone, and hashed copies on our server, so you stay signed in to the app.
  • Server logs: IP address, time and request path, for security and fraud prevention.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Cookies, cache, history and site storage of the e-GP portal; downloaded files (Downloads/e-GP); app settings. The app never stores your e-GP password. Everything you type or upload in the browser goes directly to www.eprocure.gov.bd and is governed by the portal's own policies. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

3. Who processes data for us

  • Google: "Sign in with Google" and Safe Browsing (in Android System WebView).
  • PayStation (paystation.com.bd): payment processing under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Neon (neon.tech): hosts our PostgreSQL database (AWS Singapore region).
  • Our application hosting provider, which runs the server: see https://egp-browser-site.onrender.com.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Retention

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Payment records: as long as required for accounting and tax purposes; after account deletion they are kept without the link to your account.
  • Trial hashes: up to 24 months, to prevent repeated trials.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

5. Deleting your account

Open Account & access → Delete account in the app, or request deletion on our website (https://egp-browser-site.onrender.com/legal/delete-account.html) or by email to egpwinbd@gmail.com from the address of your account. Deletion removes your account, sessions and access immediately. Lifetime access bought with a deleted account cannot be restored.

6. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your phone with a key held in Android's hardware-backed keystore where available. Payments are confirmed only by our server directly with PayStation.

7. Your choices

You can use the e-GP portal without this app in any web browser. You can request a copy or correction of your data at egpwinbd@gmail.com.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data. Contact: egpwinbd@gmail.com · https://egp-browser-site.onrender.com
