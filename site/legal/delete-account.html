Delete your e-GP Browser account

In the app: open the menu → Account & access → Delete account. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app: e-mail egpwinbd@gmail.com from the Google account you use in the app, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • Your account: Google account identifier, e-mail address and name
  • Your sign-in sessions
  • Your trial and lifetime access (a lifetime purchase cannot be restored after deletion)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records (invoice, amount, date, PayStation transaction ID), without your name or e-mail, for as long as accounting and tax rules require
  • A one-way keyed hash of your Google identifier and device, for up to 24 months, only to prevent repeated free trials
